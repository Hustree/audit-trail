# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainer via a GitHub issue or by email (see the profile/commit history).
Maintainers will review and respond as appropriate, and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
